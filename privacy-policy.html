Privacy Policy

Guia de Tendências · Last updated: October 24, 2026

Who we are

Guia de Tendências is a small personal project, run by an individual in Brazil. It selects product ideas and deals from Mercado Livre and shares them as Pins on Pinterest. You can reach us at thiagoframos88@gmail.com.

This website

This website has no accounts, forms, comments, cookies or analytics, and we do not collect personal data from its visitors.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may process technical data such as IP addresses and browser information under its own privacy statement.

Our Pinterest publishing tool

We use a private tool, built on the Pinterest API, to publish Pins to our own Pinterest account. It is used only by the owner of Guia de Tendências, and it is not offered to other people.

  • What it accesses: only the owner’s own Pinterest boards and Pins, with the owner’s authorization. It creates Pins made of an image, a title, a description and a link.
  • What it does not do: it does not access, collect, store, sell or share data from any other Pinterest user.
  • Access tokens: they are stored locally on the owner’s own device and are never shared. The owner can revoke the app’s access at any time from the Pinterest account settings.

Third-party links

Our Pins link to products on Mercado Livre, and some of these links are affiliate links: if you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Once you leave Pinterest or this website, the privacy policies of those services apply.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
